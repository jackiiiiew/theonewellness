Core Team

Chunyan Xu (Cherry)

Co-founder

Cherry brings twenty years of teaching experience at a leading (211) university in Beijing and was a visiting scholar at Duke University. Since 2014 she has practiced seated meditation and breathwork, and has systematically studied traditional Chinese medicine techniques including paida, gua sha, and acupuncture. She is a provincial-level intangible cultural heritage inheritor of the Ye Family Heart-Method Acupuncture and an inheritor of the Daoist medicine Bian Que daoyin anqiao technique. Her work focuses on mind–body co-regulation, traditional daoyin, and acupuncture therapy, exploring the integration of educational growth and Eastern wellness.

Qing Zheng (Julia)

Sanjiao Tea Ceremony

About the Sanjiao Tea Ceremony

About the Founder: Master Jianping Wang

  • Renowned ambassador of Chinese tea culture
  • Founder of the Sanjiao tea ceremony
  • Distinguished tea-culture lecturer at the Palace Museum School
  • Owner of Jundao Tea House
Master Wang's great vow: to help 100 million people love themselves through the Sanjiao tea ceremony, live to the "tea age" of 108, and reach the end of life free of illness.

Where does the Sanjiao tea ceremony come from?

Master Wang combined the habits of modern life with his understanding of the movement of qi, his knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine, and his insight into the body's meridian pathways — and, most importantly, the understanding of tea handed down from the ancients together with his own. Like refining an elixir, he distilled everything he had learned, and over half a year created the Sanjiao tea ceremony.

The practice teaches you to experience the wonderful sensation of tea's energy moving through the body. Together with drinking the Sanjiao wellness tea he blends himself, it rebuilds your body's system of awareness: one sip of tea and the energy reaches the soles of your feet — they grow warm, and your toes begin to tingle. A real, tangible experience.

What it is

  • A simple, efficient method — easy to learn and keep up — that quickly and effectively helps restore sub-health conditions;
  • Fine tea specially blended by Master Wang, brewed directly without rinsing and tested to pesticide-residue standards far stricter than Japan's, serving as an energetic catalyst to help open the Sanjiao (Triple Burner);
  • A community built on mutual trust, positive energy, and love;
  • A tool for training awareness and bringing spirit, soul, mind, intention, and will back into alignment.

What it is not

  • Not direct selling or a pyramid scheme;
  • Not a religious or spiritual organization;
  • It does not treat disease — if you are ill, see a doctor; it does not reject Western or Chinese medical treatment;
  • Not part of the "mind-body-spirit" scene.
