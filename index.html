Sanjiao Tea Ceremony

About the Sanjiao Tea Ceremony

About the Founder: Master Jianping Wang

Master Jianping Wang is a renowned ambassador of Chinese tea culture and the founder of the Sanjiao tea ceremony. He serves as a distinguished tea-culture lecturer at the Palace Museum School and is the owner of Jundao Tea House, having devoted decades to preserving and sharing the heritage of Chinese tea.

Master Wang's great vow: to help 100 million people love themselves through the Sanjiao tea ceremony, live to the "tea age" of 108, and reach the end of life free of illness.

Where does the Sanjiao tea ceremony come from?

Master Wang combined the habits of modern life with his understanding of the movement of qi, his knowledge of traditional Chinese medicine, and his insight into the body's meridian pathways — and, most importantly, the understanding of tea handed down from the ancients together with his own. Like refining an elixir, he distilled everything he had learned, and over half a year created the Sanjiao tea ceremony.

The practice teaches you to experience the wonderful sensation of tea's energy moving through the body. Together with drinking the Sanjiao wellness tea he blends himself, it rebuilds your body's system of awareness: one sip of tea and the energy reaches the soles of your feet — they grow warm, and your toes begin to tingle. A real, tangible experience.

What it is

At its heart, the Sanjiao tea ceremony is a method for restoring sub-health conditions — simple, efficient, easy to learn and easy to keep up, and quick to bring noticeable results. It is also fine tea: specially blended by Master Wang, brewed directly without rinsing, and tested to pesticide-residue standards far stricter than Japan's, the tea serves as an energetic catalyst that helps open the Sanjiao (Triple Burner).

Beyond the method and the tea, it is a community built on mutual trust, positive energy, and love — and a tool for training awareness, bringing spirit, soul, mind, intention, and will back into alignment.

What it is not

The Sanjiao tea ceremony is not direct selling or a pyramid scheme, nor a religious or spiritual organization, and it is not part of the "mind-body-spirit" scene.

It is also important to note that the practice does not treat disease — if you are ill, please see a doctor. It does not reject Western or Chinese medical treatment; the tea practice can go hand in hand with proper medical care.

Core Principles

The practice rests on three core principles: sinking the qi to the dantian, stretching and lengthening the spine, and maintaining awareness. As you drink, let the breath settle naturally into the lower dantian, allow the spine to lengthen as the body relaxes, and stay clearly aware throughout.

Results of Sustained Practice

With sustained practice, health returns to body and mind, joy arises naturally from the inside out, and many practitioners even describe a renewed, more youthful state of being — as if time were turning back.

Tips to Quick Results

The tips to quick results are simple: practice consistently, follow the guidance, and listen to fellow practitioners' sharing and the response of Master Wang. Day-by-day persistence, together with an open heart, lets the results come naturally.

Julia Zheng

Sept 8, 2026

  • #Lumbar disc degeneration
  • #Cervical spine
  • #Thoracic spine
  • #Atrial fibrillation
  • #Chronic pharyngitis
  • #Knee pain
  • #Constipation
  • #Insomnia
  • #Dry, rough skin

For seven full years my body was held hostage by severe degeneration of the lumbar discs. At my worst I could not sit, stand, walk or drive for more than fifteen minutes. Even before the pandemic I was confined at home, spending most of every day on a yoga mat or in bed. Over those seven years I tried seventeen different approaches — Eastern and Western, traditional and modern, the practical and the esoteric. Either they did nothing, or they helped a little and then stalled. Not until June 3, 2024, when I encountered the Sanjiao wellness tea ceremony (the "tea practice"), did I truly see daylight.

I have now practiced for 27 months, essentially twice a day, and body and mind have been awakened little by little: circulation and metabolism are stirred, self-healing capacity is activated, cell renewal speeds up. Ailments large and small have clearly improved, and some have disappeared altogether. Listening to fellow practitioners share and to Master Wang's responses, my inner growth has been just as quick. A life that had shrunk away has blossomed again.

Around day 100 my neck was the first to respond. At first, with the morning's first cup, I could not tilt my head back at all — the pain was severe. By the tenth cup my neck was much looser and I could look up; at about three months I could tilt my head back easily on the very first sip, with no pain at all. I had a quiet sense that the practice would repair me in order: cervical spine, then thoracic, then lumbar.

At thirteen months the thoracic spine began to repair, and it continued for about two months. With each exhale there was pain on the right side of the thoracic spine — as I understand it, qi pushing against the lesion. Gradually, sneezing no longer meant back pain and leaking urine, and my bones no longer ached and flared in damp weather. In the autumn and winter of 2025 I visited twelve cities in four months — family, friends, travel, classes — leaving behind for good the life of being trapped at home.

At twenty-four months the lumbar repair reached another level. Cooking, gatherings with friends, going out — seven or eight hours in a row without needing to lie down. My quality of life and my whole state of being have turned around completely.

Other changes in body and mind have been just as clear:

  • Atrial fibrillation: from six Holter monitor tests in eight months, with episodes even on medication, to a year and a half of stability on my regular prescription — no more trips to the hospital;
  • Chronic pharyngitis improved; my qi is fuller, my voice is strong, and I can speak in long sentences again;
  • The knee I injured playing volleyball in my youth no longer hurts;
  • My heels no longer crack in winter, and rough skin has become smooth;
  • Constipation improved — from small white pellets to well-formed golden stools;
  • Sleep improved; I sleep through the night;
  • My eyes are bright, age spots have faded, my complexion has gone from dark and dull to rosy, and muscle has come back to my forearms and thighs;
  • Awareness has grown: less duality and judgement, more smiles; a heart that is softer, more relaxed and more playful; less fear, able to take a joke; one persona after another dropped away; from weighing every word to speaking freely; able to let small irritations out as they arise — and my relationship with my husband has improved wonderfully.

My deep gratitude to Master Jianping Wang and to the Sanjiao wellness tea ceremony he created. Beyond what it has done for my body, the daily companionship and response have grown my character — and that is the root of it all.

I want to pass this love and blessing on, so that the countless people who, like me, struggle with illness and search endlessly for a cure can take fewer wrong turns, find this practice sooner, benefit sooner, take back ownership of their health and their awareness, and let life blossom again.

Mary Yin

  • #Depression
  • #Insomnia
  • #Medication side effects

My connection with the Sanjiao tea ceremony practice began on December 8, 2023, in the Wuyi Mountains. At that time I was struggling with moderate depression and depended on medication to get through the day, and the side effects were deeply affecting my life and my work. I soon realized that something had to change, so I decided to try stopping the medication and instead joined the Sanjiao practice every morning at six o'clock.

Before I knew it, change had quietly taken hold. I began to sleep soundly for about four hours, and even after waking I could stay in a state of deep, restful relaxation. Little by little, life and work returned to their original course. At a gathering in 2024 I met a German doctor of biomedicine who measured my life energy with his instrument. The reading suggested that my depressive state was being held steadily in check by some kind of practice. Curious, he asked what I practiced. I told him: the Sanjiao tea ceremony — and that I drink Sanjiao tea every morning and join the six o'clock online practice group.

I have shared this experience many times. Fellow tea practitioners have asked me how I came out of depression without medication. My answer is always the same: "Keep up the Sanjiao practice, and be sure to drink the Sanjiao tea along with it." To this day I have been practicing and drinking Sanjiao tea for more than two years. My life now feels both sunlit and gentle — clear, steady, and full of inner strength.
